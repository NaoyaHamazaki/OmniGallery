How it works

  1. Choose a typeWall work / sculpture
  2. UploadFormat checked automatically
  3. DetailsTitle, artist, description
  4. PlacementPublic or private room
  5. ReviewContent and rights check
  6. On displayWe let you know by email

Gallery rooms

Public rooms

The museum's main galleries, open to every visitor. Works are shown after review by our team.

Private room

Your own small room, open only to people who know its room code. Enter it with the PRIVATE button in the museum's elevator.

Guidelines

  • Only works you own the rights to, or have permission to show. No characters, logos or other people's works without permission.
  • Sexual, violent, discriminatory or otherwise offensive content cannot be shown.
  • Location data and other metadata are removed automatically when your file is converted for display.
  • Review results and the start of your exhibition are sent to the email address you register.
